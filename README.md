## What the Add-On Does

ChronoCraft Adobe Express Add-On is a compact integration designed to carry ChronoCraft-generated material into Adobe Express. Its workflow is centered on infographic documents, giving users a practical route for bringing content into a visual design project.

The project follows a Node.js-based add-on structure and supports a smoother handoff between generated content and Adobe Express. Once imported, the material can be organized and polished in the design environment without relying on unnecessary intermediate steps.
